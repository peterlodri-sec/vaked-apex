VAKED Whitepaper

A fair-launch, proof-of-work-minted utility token of the vaked.dev constellation.

Not an investment. VAKED is a utility token for proof of presence and participation. It confers no financial rights, no profit participation, no redemption, and no promise of value. Its price may go to zero. Acquire it only to participate in the vaked.dev constellation.

2. What VAKED is (and is not)

VAKED is a fungible utility token on Polygon PoS. Its only function is proof of presence and participation in the vaked.dev constellation — a digital "I was here / I contributed" attestation that gates access to constellation resources (the Sovereign Commons).

The first utility: the Sovereign Library

VAKED's first concrete use is the Sovereign Library (pocoo.vaked.dev) — the free 37-volume book catalog. The books remain free to read (no paywall, no DRM). VAKED adds the attestation layer on top: a gasless proof-of-presence stamp that records "I read / I held / I participated" in the library, verifiable on-chain, without charging anyone for knowledge.

  • Read — the books stay open to everyone.
  • Attest — a presence stamp proves you were there.
  • Hold — VAKED is earned by mining, not bought from the issuer.

VAKED is not:

  • an investment, equity, or debt instrument;
  • a claim on any revenue, profit, dividend, or treasury;
  • a promise of any future value or appreciation;
  • a MiFID financial instrument.

There are no financial rights attached to holding VAKED. No one — including the issuer — is obligated to buy it back, list it, or support a market price.

3. Underlying technology

  • Standard: ERC-20 (fungible), deployed on Polygon PoS (EVM) at 0x2Ae7DA713A2c8527AF70825C0F79632AF2e2ae4A.
  • Minting: proof-of-work, EIP-918-style — miners find a nonce such that keccak256(challengeNumber ‖ msg.sender ‖ nonce) < miningTarget. Solutions are bound to the miner's address and to a rotating round challenge that persists across blocks, so work cannot be replayed or relayed between miners.
  • Difficulty: auto-adjusts every 1,024 mints toward a target average 60-block mint interval. Initial target 1<<232, clamped rails [1<<200, 1<<232], midpoint smoothing (±4× per epoch, never all-or-nothing).
  • Rewards: start 50 VAKED/mint, halved every 210,000 mints. The geometric series converges on the 21,000,000 VAKED hard cap.

4. Distribution — fair launch (verifiable, not promised)

VAKED is only ever minted by proof of work. There is:

  • no premine,
  • no team allocation,
  • no presale,
  • no airdrop pool,
  • no issuer-held reserve.

Ownership of the contract is renounced to the zero address — the contract is now immutable, so the issuer cannot mint, pause, freeze, tax, or change it. Source is published and verified to match the deployed bytecode on Sourcify and Polygonscan.

Verifiable on-chain. These are engineering facts, not promises: owner() == address(0), no mint function beyond the PoW mint(uint256 nonce), and a fixed 21,000,000 cap — all checkable against the published source.

5. Offer terms

  • Offer type: no sale by the issuer — tokens are earned by performing proof of work.
  • Consideration: none to the issuer (miners pay only network gas).
  • Maximum supply: 21,000,000 VAKED.
  • Jurisdiction: the issuer offers to EU/EEA persons under MiCA Title II as applicable; persons in restricted jurisdictions should not participate.

6. Rights and obligations of holders

Holders have the right to hold and transfer VAKED and to use it as proof of presence / participation in the constellation. Holders have no governance, economic, redemption, or claim rights against the issuer.

7. Risks

  • Loss of value: VAKED may become worthless; there is no floor.
  • No liquidity: there is no issuer-provided market; it may be impossible to sell.
  • Regulatory risk: treatment of utility/mineable tokens varies by jurisdiction and may change.
  • Smart-contract risk: bugs, exploits, and irreversible loss are possible; ownership is renounced, so no fix is possible post-launch.
  • Network risk: Polygon PoS and its RPC infrastructure may change; gas and availability are outside the issuer's control.
  • MEV / sniping: mining and transfer activity is public and can be front-run.
