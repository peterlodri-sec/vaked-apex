Story

vaked.dev began as a refusal — a refusal to let knowledge, art, and compute be enclosed behind paywalls and black boxes.

The constellation grew node by node: a sovereign library of free volumes, a generative art engine, a music synth, a math notebook, and autonomous agents — each its own repository, its own frontier, none of it gated.

Along the way a question kept recurring: how do you attest — prove that someone was there, that a work is what it claims, that a contribution happened — without turning knowledge into a product? The answer became VAKED.

VAKED is the constellation's accounting layer: a proof-of-work utility token that attests presence and participation without charging anyone for knowledge. The books stay free; VAKED is the cryptographic stamp on top — "I was here, I read this, I contributed" — verifiable on-chain.

It launched on Polygon in 2026 as a fair launch: no premine, no team allocation, no presale, no issuer-held reserve. The first 21,000,000 VAKED are mined entirely by anyone willing to solve the challenge — one 50-VAKED reward at a time, halving every 210,000 mints, until the cap is reached.

That's the whole story so far: a commons that earns its own attestation, in the open.
