About

vaked.dev is the apex of the vaked constellation — a sovereign mesh of art, music, books, mathematics, and machine intelligence. Every surface is its own project, its own repository, its own deployment; the apex is the shared front door.

The surfaces

  • pocoo.vaked.dev — the Sovereign Library: free volumes, no paywall, no DRM, on-chain presence stamps.
  • music.vaked.dev — generative ambient audio and a living sound layer.
  • art.vaked.dev — generative art and a secret NFT.
  • store.vaked.dev — physical store and proof-of-presence masters.
  • worklog.vaked.dev — the fleet board.
  • ocean.vaked.dev — high-compute and memory engine.
  • …and the rest of the constellation.

VAKED

VAKED is a fair-launch, proof-of-work-minted utility token on Polygon — the constellation's sovereign accounting for presence, provenance, and contribution. It is earned by solving verifiable cryptographic challenges, not bought from an issuer. No premine, no team allocation, fixed 21,000,000 supply. Read the whitepaper.

Who

Values

  • Open source — MIT-licensed code, published and verifiable.
  • Sovereign — no enclosure of knowledge; the books stay free.
  • Verifiable, not promised — claims are checkable on-chain and against the published source.
