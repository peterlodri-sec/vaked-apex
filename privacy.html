Privacy

vaked.dev is a static site. We run no accounts, no login, no tracking cookies, and no advertising. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

What touches this site

  • Edge logs — the static host (Cloudflare) may see your IP address in its server logs, used solely to serve the pages and prevent abuse. We do not read or retain these for tracking.
  • Email — if you write to us, we use your email only to reply.

On-chain activity is public

VAKED mints, transfers, and attestations happen on-chain on Polygon and are public by design — visible to anyone, including block explorers. This is inherent to the token, not a tracking choice.

Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. For any privacy question, contact peter.lodri@gmail.com.
